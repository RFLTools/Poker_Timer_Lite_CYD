In tournament poker, blinds increase on a set schedule — every 10, 15, or 20 minutes the stakes go up. A blind timer automates that clock: it shows the current level, announces when blinds are about to rise, and keeps the whole table informed so the host can deal instead of watching a stopwatch.

The Poker Timer puts that clock in a dedicated touchscreen device built for your home game. No phone apps, no laptop, no squinting across the room. Tap START and it runs the tournament from the first blind to the final hand — levelling up automatically whether you're on a single table or syncing wirelessly across multiple tables, and sounding the buzzer whenever the blinds are about to go up.
